# Quality standard

A diagram is deliverable when:
- no critical overlaps or out-of-bounds elements remain;
- body text remains readable at target output size;
- no section is visually overloaded compared with adjacent sections;
- colors are semantic and consistent;
- headings and body copy follow a clear hierarchy;
- arrows communicate flow without decorative clutter;
- both diagrams share a visual language but retain independent information architecture;
- SVG remains the source of truth;
- PNG/PDF are derived from the same SVG;
- the package includes model/spec/config/QA/editor for traceability and adjustment.
